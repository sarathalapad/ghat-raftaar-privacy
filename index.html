Ghat Raftaar Privacy Policy

EAOP Games · Effective date: 30 October 2026

Summary

  • Ghat Raftaar is a hill-climb driving game. You do not need an account, a login, an email address or a phone number to play.
  • The game itself does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We run no servers that receive your game data.
  • The game needs an internet connection to play. To check the connection it contacts a Google connectivity endpoint.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised, and the ad SDK can use the Android Advertising ID. Where a consent message applies, your choice decides what is allowed.
  • There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no way for players to talk to each other.

1. Who we are

Ghat Raftaar (Android package com.eaop.ghatraftaar) is published under the developer name EAOP Games by SarathKumar.K ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps the following on your device only: coins, gems and experience; vehicles and regions you have unlocked and selected; vehicle and part upgrade levels and equipped parts; best distances, cup results and trophies; recordings of your own best runs (used as ghost cars); daily task, chest, event and season progress; computer-controlled rival and club progress; the number of runs played; and your settings (language, music, sound).

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the game turns off Android cloud backup for it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it permanently.

3. Internet connection check

Ghat Raftaar needs the internet to play and pauses with a message when the device is offline. To check this, the game sends a simple request to a Google connectivity endpoint (https://clients3.google.com/generate_204) every few seconds. The request carries no game data and no identifiers from us. Like any web request, it is received with your device's IP address and basic technical information, which Google handles under the Google Privacy Policy. The game also uses Android's network-state permission to know whether a connection is available.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ghat Raftaar is free and is supported by ads delivered through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, by Google LLC). No other ad network or mediation SDK is used.

How ads appear

  • A full-screen ad appears each time the game starts, including the first launch. It does not appear when you return to the game from the background. If it cannot load within a few seconds, the game simply continues.
  • A full-screen ad appears after a run or race ends, never while you are driving. The game does not wait for this ad to load: if none is ready, play simply continues. It is skipped if you finished watching a rewarded ad less than 30 seconds earlier.
  • A banner ad is shown on screen at the top of the game, including while you drive. It is hidden only while a full-screen ad is showing.
  • Optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch ads instead of paying for things (for example "watch 2 ads" for a reward). Nothing is granted for an ad you do not finish; ads you finish count toward the reward. There is nothing to buy with real money.

How ads are configured

  • Ads may be personalised. This means Google and its advertising partners may use the Advertising ID and information about activity in apps and on websites to choose ads they expect to interest you, and to measure them.
  • Ad content is limited to the "T" (teen) maximum content rating.
  • The game includes the Android Advertising ID permission and the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions that come with the ad SDK, so the SDK can use them. The game's own code does not read or store the identifier. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID, and manage Privacy Sandbox ad settings where your device has them, in Android Settings. The menu names vary by device.
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) may be shown before ads load, and your choice decides whether ads may be personalised or shown; if so, an 'Ad privacy choices' button appears in Settings so you can change your choice. Some US states give residents rights over the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising, and personalised ads may fall under those terms. Where no consent message applies, you may not be asked, and ads may be personalised.

What the ad SDK may process

To deliver, personalise and measure ads, count impressions, cap how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK and ad networks may automatically process information such as the Advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), device make and model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app version, and ad interaction events (for example that an ad was shown, closed or clicked), plus diagnostics about the SDK itself. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login, and no request for personal information.
  • No in-app purchases or real-money payments, no chat, no friend lists and no user-generated content.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK of our own, and no servers of our own that receive your data.

7. Children's privacy

Ghat Raftaar is made for players aged 13 and over. The game is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask for your age. If you believe a child under 13 is playing or has given us personal information (for example by emailing us), contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com and we will delete any such information we hold. A parent or guardian can also reset or delete the device's Advertising ID and uninstall the game.

8. Data deletion and your rights

  • All game data lives on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings, removes it completely. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
  • Data processed by Google for ads or the connection check is handled under Google's own retention rules. You can manage Google's ad settings for your device in your Google account and Android settings.
  • Parents, guardians and players can ask us any question about privacy, or ask us to delete any email they sent us, at alapadsarath@gmail.com.

9. Security

The connection check and all ad traffic use encrypted HTTPS connections. Game progress is kept in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change adds any new kind of data collection, we will also update the Google Play listing and, where the law requires it, ask for consent first.
